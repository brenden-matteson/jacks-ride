JacksRide Wireframe

Figure 1: Wireframe outlining potential design of JacksRide

This wireframe was designed in an environmental studies capstone paper titled "Clear the Road: Long Distance Transportation Alternatives for the NAU Community," by Trey Kettering, Noël Rooney, and Raquel Martinez. The wireframe outlines a potential workflow of account registration that we will integrate into JacksRide
