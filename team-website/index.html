What is JacksRide?

JacksRide is a rideshare service platform that helps NAU students save money on long distance travel by providing a secure platform to facilitate carpooling between students.

Our Proposition

We plan to build a web application accessed through NAUgo where users can create rider or driver profiles and request or submit rides to the forum. If we can have more students carpool, that will in turn reduce carbon emissions through the trips saved.
