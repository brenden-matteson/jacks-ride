    The Problem

    Our clients have seen how many cars sit idle at NAU for the whole semester and only go anywhere for weekend trips or to go home at the end of a semester, with only one person per car, which raises gas emissions. Meanwhile, students without cars need to find ways to travel long distances to either see family/friends or attend events or they might feel left out. Common options include purchasing a ticket to ride a shuttle, using ridesharing apps like Uber or Lyft, and using social media platforms to contact other students for rides. However, these options come with different restrictions or limitations that are placed on the student: high prices, relying on non-students for travel, unreliable platforms to reach other students, to name a few.

    What is JacksRide?

    JacksRide is a rideshare service platform that helps NAU students save money on long distance travel by providing a secure platform to facilitate carpooling between students.

    The Solution

    JacksRide was created by another capstone group as an idea to not only solve gas emissions but also help students to find cheaper options for travel. The goal for Blue and Gold Transit Collective is to create this idea into reality. JacksRide will act as a forum where students will log into their NAU account, via NAU CAS Login, and create either a driver profile or rider profile. After setting up, the student can now find rides hosted by other students. Drivers can put up a post explaining where they are going for this trip and allow other students to ride with them, with set conditions of course (price being the main one). Riders can either make their own post, asking Drivers if they are going to their desired location and can provide information on what they can pitch in, or see any Driver's post and request to join their ride. All conversations between students will be in private direct messages. Similarly, students can discuss payment, pick-up location, and other concerns they might have. At the end of the rides, students can rate each other to help not only build a good image (either as a driver or rider), but also give new riders a better understanding of which drivers to pick. All of this will be hosted on a web application within NAUgo app.

    Our Vision

    We plan to build a web application accessed through NAUgo where users can create rider or driver profiles and request or submit rides to the forum. We plan to build private messaging into the platform to facilitate communcation between drivers and riders.

    Requirements

    • Students can login with NAUID.

    • Students can communicate with one another securely.

    • There is a rating system for students to rate one another for when things go well or students can know about it before they have someone in their car or get in someone else's.

    • Students are able to post on a public forum about plans to drive or a request to be driven.

    • The application is accessible through or located on the NAUGO app/website.

    • Users can interact with public posts to accept offers or offer rides to those asking.

    Development Process

    • Define all requirements and talk with client about the requirements they have.

    • Design a basic UI to allow for implementation of main features.

    • Use NAU's database of Student ID's to see who is allowed to login.

    • Create the main feature of students being able to communicate in public forums, and private DMs.

    • Newest posts on top, old ones go towards the bottom.

    • Update UI to be more visually appealing and on-brand for the school.

    • Connect our application to the NAUGO app or simply make it accessible through the app

    The Impact

    If we can have more students carpool, that will in turn reduce carbon emissions through the trips saved.
